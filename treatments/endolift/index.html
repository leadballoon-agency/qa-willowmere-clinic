Is Endolift right for you?

Who it's for

Endolift suits mild to moderate skin laxity along the jawline, jowls, under the chin and neck, and puffiness under the eyes. It isn't a replacement for a facelift when skin laxity is severe; Dr Shah will tell you honestly at your consultation.

Questions

Does it hurt? We use local anaesthetic, so most people feel pressure and warmth rather than pain.

How long is recovery? Most people have one to two days of swelling or bruising. Some take up to a week.

When will I see results? Some tightening straight away, with results developing over three months as collagen builds.

How long do results last? Results vary from person to person; your practitioner will talk you through what to expect.
